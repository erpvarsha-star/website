## 3. Search targets (own these intents)

Priority keyword clusters, mapped to pages:
1. **Sourcing intent:** "forging company India", "forging manufacturer India", "sourcing forgings from India", "closed die forging India", "forging supplier India OEM" → homepage + /forging-manufacturer-india + a new "Sourcing Forgings from India" guide page.
2. **Capability intent:** "alloy steel forgings India", "forged and machined components supplier", "forging machining assembly one supplier" → /alloy-steel-forgings, /forged-machined-components-vf, /assembly-services.
3. **Product intent:** "forged steering components", "suspension forgings India", "V-link assemblies manufacturer", "radius rod / torque rod forgings" → product pages.
4. **Industry intent:** "railway forgings India RDSO", "agricultural equipment forgings", "oil and gas forgings India", "EV suspension components India" → industry pages.
5. **Buyer-process intent (blog, highest GEO value):** "how to source forgings from India", "forging vs casting", "PPAP levels explained", "how to read a forging drawing", "India vs China forging cost", "what to ask a forging supplier".

GEO specifics: AI engines cite pages that answer a question in the first 100 words, carry FAQ schema, show dates, and are corroborated off-site. Every new page/post follows the W6 pattern: question-led H1, direct answer first, H2 sections, 3-question FAQ, dateModified in schema.

## 5. Beyond SEO/GEO — the weekly business-growth plan

Positioning (use everywhere, one sentence): **"Varsha Forgings — closed-die forgings, precision machining and assembly from one Indian supplier, 1–25 kg, IATF 16949, PPAP L3, since 1989."**

### LinkedIn (2 posts/week, owner face + company page)
- Tue: engineering proof — a part photo with the tolerance/material story (never customer names without consent).
- Thu: buyer education — one sourcing tip from the blog, linking the site.
- Monthly: award/certification/company-milestone post (ACMA 2020, Ashok Leyland 2023, SME100).
- Yash + Piyush comment on 5 target-account posts weekly (OEMs, Tier-1s in CV/agri/railway/EV).

### Email campaigns (2 sequences, monthly newsletter)
- **New-prospect sequence (4 emails, 2 weeks apart):** capability intro → relevant case/part story → quality & PPAP proof → RFQ ask with the 24–48 h quote promise. Audience: scraped/enriched lists of sourcing engineers at OEMs/Tier-1s (see tools).
- **Dormant-enquiry reactivation (2 emails):** every RFQ that went quiet >90 days gets a "capacity open this quarter" note.
- **Monthly newsletter:** 1 new capability page + 1 blog post + 1 plant update. Sent to all past enquirers; replies feed GA4 lead tracking.

### Lead-generation tools (pick with Yash, spend >$50 needs approval)
- **Apollo.io or Lusha** (~$50–100/mo): build lists of sourcing/procurement engineers at target OEMs and Tier-1s in India, EU, US; feeds the email sequence.
- **IndiaMART + TradeIndia paid listings:** highest-ROI channel for domestic forging buyers; also feeds off-site citations.
- **Alibaba / Kompass / ThomasNet profiles:** where overseas buyers shortlist Indian suppliers.
- **GA4 + GSC + Sited (already owned):** measurement stack, no new cost.
- Optional later: Windsor.ai for AI-referral attribution; a simple CRM (HubSpot free) once enquiry volume justifies it.

### Trade shows & associations (quarterly)
- ACMA events, IMTEX, Hannover Messe (with an India pavilion), Bauma (construction/agri buyers). Goal: 2 shows/year walked, 1 exhibited when pipeline supports it.

### Weekly operating rhythm (the standing Monday task)
1. KPI strip: GSC clicks/impressions for the §3 clusters · GA4 AI-referral visits · Wix form leads · Sited prompt visibility (monthly).
2. Ship: 1 blog post or 1 deepened page (alternating), LinkedIn posts scheduled, email batch sent.
3. Check: pending.md unblocks, ssr-check clean, no fact drift (banned-term scan).
4. Log everything in website.md; refresh handover.
