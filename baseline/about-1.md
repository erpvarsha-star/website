Closed Die Forging. Closed die forging is a manufacturing process that involves shaping metal by striking it with a hammer or press within a closed die. This method allows precise control over the dimensions and properties of the final product, in high-strength components that are often used in critical applications. With its ability to produce complex shapes and reduce waste, die forging is a preferred choice in industries such as automotive and oil and gas.
