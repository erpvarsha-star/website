Contract Assembly & Sub-Assembly Services for OEM Line-Ready Delivery. Varsha Forgings provides value-added assembly and sub-assembly services that transform individually forged and machined components into installation-ready modules for OEM assembly lines. Operating from the Pune (Markal, Khed) facility, these services eliminate the need for OEMs to manage multiple Tier-2 vendors for hardware, elastomeric bushes, fasteners, and sub-assembly validation. By integrating assembly operations under the same quality system that governs forging and machining, Varsha Forgings ensures dimensional consistency, torque specification compliance, and functional validation across every module delivered. This capability is particularly critical for suspension linkage assemblies, steering sub-systems, and railway bogie modules where improper hardware torque or bushing alignment can result in field failures.
