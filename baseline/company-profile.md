Leadership. Mr. Jinendra Munot. Chairman. Mr. Jinendra Munot is a distinguished industrialist and the visionary Chairman of Varsha Forgings Private Limited. With a Masters in Mechanical & Production Engineering from the University of Washington, Seattle, he returned to India in 1976 to join his uncle, Mr. Firodia, at Jaya Hind Industries / Force Motors Ltd. In 1981, Mr. Munot, in collaboration with Mr. Firodia, co-founded ZF Steering Gear Limited in Pune, a joint venture with ZF AG (now Bosch Automotive Steering), aimed at manufacturing mechanical and hydraulic power steering gears in India. This venture helped lay the foundation for his long-standing impact on the automotive components industry. In 2005, Mr. Munot established Prima Feintechnik LLP, with a focus on acquiring auto component companies and setting up an industrial business park, M Park LLP. That same year, he acquired Varsha Forgings Private Limited, a company specializing in hot forging components for the automotive industry. Under his leadership, Varsha Forgings quickly expanded its capabilities, becoming a trusted supplier to leading automotive manufacturers. Mr. Munot's technical acumen and forward-thinking approach were key to Varsha Forgings' success. In 2007, the company expanded its footprint by establishing a plant in Pune to manufacture suspension linkages for MAN Trucks India. His commitment to innovation led to a joint venture in 2013 with Korea Central Corporation (KCTR), now T&G Corporation Korea, to produce steering and chassis linkages for Tata Motors and Mahindra commercial vehicles. Known for his engineering mindset and as a technocrat, Mr. Munot has always been driven by the desire to stay ahead of changing demands in the automotive industry. Mr. Yash J Munot. CEO & Director. Yash J Munot, a seasoned business leader with over two decades of experience, holds a BA Hons in Business Management & Finance from Regents Business School, UK (2001).
