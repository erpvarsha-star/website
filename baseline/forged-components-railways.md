Forged Components for Railway Rolling Stock & Bogie Systems. Railway rolling stock operates under extreme load cycles, continuous vibration, impact forces, and environmental exposure across long service lives. These demanding requirements make forged components the preferred choice for safety-critical railway applications due to their superior fatigue resistance, structural integrity, and resistance to catastrophic brittle fracture. Varsha Forgings manufactures forged, machined, and assembled components for railway applications from its IATF 16949-certified facilities in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) and Pune. The company supplies bogie guide links, traction rods, brake levers, drawbar hooks, damper brackets, and anti-roll suspension connections for passenger and freight rail networks operating under Indian Railways (RDSO) standards.
