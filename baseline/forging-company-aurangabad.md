## First 300 words (visible body)
Heavy-Die Forging & Metallurgy Hub. The Varsha Forgings Aurangabad Division is the company's primary metallurgical and heavy closed-die forging facility. Located in MIDC Waluj, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra, this plant has been operational since 1989 and forms the metallurgical backbone of the organization's manufacturing ecosystem. Unlike precision-machining or assembly-focused facilities, the Aurangabad plant is engineered for primary shape formation of high-strength alloy and carbon steel components. It houses heavy hydraulic press lines, friction drop hammers, an integrated die manufacturing shop, continuous-feed heat treatment furnaces, and a fully equipped metallurgical laboratory. This concentration of heavy forging infrastructure makes it the designated production node for structural automotive, railway, and commercial vehicle forgings ranging from 1-25 kg. Aurangabad Plant Layout: Induction Billet Heating, Descaling, 2500T Press Line, 1300T Dual Press Lines, Die Tool Room, Flash Trimming, Hammer Shop 1-3 MT, Shot Blasting, Heat Treatment, Metallurgy Lab, MPI / Hardness, Normalizing / Q&T. Output: near-net-shape forgings to Pune (machining and assembly). Engineering Specifications: Location (Plot No. B-7, MIDC Waluj, Maharashtra 431136), Year Established 1989, Quality Certification IATF 16949 and ISO 9001:2015, Component Weight Range 0.5 kg to 50 kg, Operating Hours Monday-Saturday 08:00-18:00 IST.
