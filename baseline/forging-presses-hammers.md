Precision Forging Presses & Hammer Capacities | IATF 16949 Certified. Varsha Forgings runs two IATF 16949 and ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing plants in India: Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), established in 1989, and Pune, established in 2007. Across both sites, the shop floor covers hot closed-die forging for components from 1-25 kg, in material grades including EN8, EN9, EN19, EN24, EN31, 20MnCr5, SAE 8620, AISI 4140, and AISI 1045. Verified Shop-Floor Equipment (table): Erie Press Line 2,500 Ton; Press Lines (Qty 2) 1,300 Ton each; Screw Press 800 Ton; Drop Hammer 3 Metric Ton; Drop Hammer 2 Metric Ton; Drop Hammer 1.5 Metric Ton; Drop Hammer 1 Metric Ton; Continuous Tube HT Furnace 1 Ton/Hour; Batch Type HT Furnaces; Electrical Tempering Furnaces; ISO Annealing Lines. Near-Net-Shape Hot Closed-Die Forging. Each forging route is selected for the part's stress profile, tolerance target, and material grade, all run as near-net-shape processes that keep metal grain flow continuous along the load path for strong fatigue resistance. Certified Quality Assurance and Engineering Validation. Every press, hammer, and furnace listed above operates under an IATF 16949 and ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system. Die design uses Creo Parametric 3.0, forming simulation uses SimuFact Forming 15.0, and BricsCAD.
