## First 300 words (visible body)
Heat Treatment & Metallurgical Validation for Forged Steel Components. Heat treatment is the critical bridge between raw forging and finished component performance. Without proper thermal processing, even perfectly forged components will exhibit inconsistent mechanical properties, residual stresses, and microstructural defects that compromise fatigue life and dimensional stability. Varsha Forgings maintains comprehensive in-house heat treatment capabilities at its Aurangabad (MIDC Waluj) facility, supported by a fully equipped metallurgical laboratory. HEAT TREATMENT PROCESS MAP: Incoming Forged Component, Pre-Clean / Degrease, Normalizing (850-900 C, air cool), Isothermal Annealing, Quenching and Tempering (820-880 C, temper 200-650 C), Stress Relieving, Post-Heat Inspection, Hardness Test, Metallurgical Sample, Shot Blasting, CMM Validation, Dispatch to Machining. Heat Treatment Processes and Specifications: 1. Normalizing (table). 2. Isothermal Annealing (table). 3. Quenching and Tempering (table). 4. Stress Relieving (table). Metallurgical Laboratory Capabilities: Chemical Analysis (OES, XRF, wet chemistry); Microstructural Analysis; Hardness Testing; Mechanical Testing; Non-Destructive Testing (MPI, UT, DPI).
