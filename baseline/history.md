Milestone. VFL, established in 1989, is a leading Indian manufacturer of hot forgings in Carbon and Alloy Steel with in-house machining for automotive and engineering applications. Renowned for high-quality, cost-effective parts, over 60% of its production serves OEMs. Equipped with advanced technology and skilled engineers, VFL focuses on process improvements.
