Custom Forged Solutions for Oil, Gas, and Industrial Infrastructure. Varsha Forgings is a premier, IATF 16949 and ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer specializing in heavy-duty forged components engineered to survive extreme operations. Our production facilities deploy closed-die press capabilities designed specifically to withstand high-pressure, cryogenic, and highly corrosive chemical environments. We supply high-integrity critical path components to global petrochemical plants, pipeline infrastructure networks, and heavy industrial machinery OEMs. Our production lines handle precision component weights from 1-25 kg using raw material stock sourced exclusively from approved, tier-one metallurgical mills. Every forging is subjected to precise thermal processing to eliminate internal micro-voids, refine steel grain boundaries, and maximize mechanical fatigue life. Structural Parameter Matrix: Material Grades Processed (Stainless Steel 304L, 316L; Carbon Steel A105, LF2; Alloy Steel F11, F22). Component Weight Limits (0.5 kg to 50 kg). Pressure Class Compliance (ASME Class 150, 300, 600, 900, 1500, up to Class 2500). In-House Heat Treatment. Machining Tolerances (CNC turning, milling, boring down to +/-0.02 mm).
