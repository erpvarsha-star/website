High-Strength Radius Rod Assemblies for Commercial Vehicles & Railway Systems. Radius rods (also referred to as radius arms, reaction arms, or track rods) are critical suspension linkages designed to control fore-aft (longitudinal) wheel and axle movement while maintaining proper suspension geometry during vertical travel. Varsha Forgings produces closed-die forged radius rod assemblies, including fixed-length, threaded adjustable, and crimped configurations, engineered to handle dynamic push/pull forces in heavy commercial vehicles, buses, and rail bogies. In multi-axle truck and trailer configurations, radius rods prevent axle shift relative to the chassis frame during hard braking and rapid acceleration. RADIUS ROD ASSEMBLY, LOAD MAP.
