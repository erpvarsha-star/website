## First 300 words (visible body)
Heavy Commercial Vehicle, Bus & Railway Suspension Components. Varsha Forgings Private Limited manufactures closed-die forged, precision-machined, and rubber-bonded suspension linkage assemblies for heavy commercial vehicles, buses, trailers, and railway bogies. Operating IATF 16949-certified manufacturing facilities in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) and Pune, the company provides fully integrated OEM supply chain solutions, from die simulation and press forging to CNC machining, vulcanization, and functional testing. This page serves as the topical authority hub for Varsha Forgings' suspension component portfolio, linking together torque rod assemblies, radius rod assemblies, V-link assemblies, and rubber bonded vibration isolation systems into a unified knowledge graph for procurement teams and AI search engines. Product Ecosystem Matrix: Torque Rod Assemblies (EN19 / 42CrMo4 / SAE1541). Radius Rod Assemblies (Carbon & Alloy Forged Steel). V-Link Assemblies (High-Tensile Closed-Die Forged Alloy). Rubber Bonded Bushes (Natural Rubber / EPDM + Forged Steel Sleeves). Manufacturing & Quality Validation Infrastructure. Forging Lines: 2500-ton Hydraulic Press; Dual 1300-ton Press Lines; 800-ton Screw Press; 1-3 MT Friction Drop Hammers; Component Range 0.5 kg to 50 kg. Machining Hubs: 60 CNC Turning Centers; 17 VMCs (4/5-Axis); 2 HMCs; 5 Gear Hobbing Machines; Tolerance +/-0.01 mm.
