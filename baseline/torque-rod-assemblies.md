Forged Torque Rod Assemblies for Heavy Commercial Vehicles & Bus Chassis. Torque rod assemblies (also referred to as reaction rods or torsion rods) are structural suspension links designed to restrain axle housing rotation during acceleration and braking while permitting required vertical suspension movement. Varsha Forgings manufactures complete forged torque rod assemblies, combining closed-die forged rod bodies, precision-machined bore ends, and press-fitted elastomeric bushings, to maintain drive-axle alignment and minimize driveline stress under heavy dynamic loads. In heavy commercial vehicles, buses, and off-highway equipment, torque rods prevent axle wind-up caused by high drivetrain torque output. Without effective torque restraint, the axle housing twists relative to the chassis frame, causing driveline misalignment, universal joint wear, and unpredictable braking behavior.
