Forged V-Link Suspension Assemblies for Heavy-Duty Applications. A V-Link assembly (also referred to as a V-stay, V-rod, or three-point linkage) is a structural suspension component that combines the functions of transverse stabilization and longitudinal positioning into a single V-shaped unit. Positioned between the vehicle frame cross-member and the axle housing, forged V-Link assemblies control lateral sway, prevent roll motion, and maintain correct axle orientation during asymmetrical suspension travel. In heavy commercial vehicles, articulated buses, and railway bogies where packaging space is constrained, the V-Link replaces separate lateral track bars (panhard rods) and upper control links. This integration reduces component count, saves chassis envelope space, and simplifies OEM assembly line operations. V-LINK ASSEMBLY, 3-POINT GEOMETRY. Central Pivot / Frame Attachment.
