## What a bogie does

A bogie is the frame assembly under a railway vehicle. It carries the vehicle weight, holds the wheelsets, and passes traction and braking forces to the body. Because it works under repeated load and vibration for years, its small connecting parts carry real responsibility.

Many of those connecting parts are steel forgings. The forging process refines the grain flow and gives a dense structure, which suits parts that take repeated load.

## Bogie components that are commonly forged

Varsha Forgings produces the following railway components:

- **Bogie guide links**, which locate the bogie frame in relation to the axle
- **Traction rods**, which pass traction and braking force between bogie and body
- **Brake levers**, which transmit the brake force through the rigging
- **Drawbar hooks**, which carry coupling loads between vehicles
- **Damper brackets**, which hold the dampers that control vibration

Each has a different load path and a different risk, so each drawing needs different emphasis.

## What to put on a bogie component drawing

A forging supplier can quote quickly when the drawing is complete. Check that yours states:

- Material grade and any heat treatment condition
- Hardness range, and where on the part it is measured
- Critical dimensions, with the datums used for inspection
- Surface requirements and any areas to be machined
- Crack testing requirements
- Marking and traceability requirements
- Any customer or railway specification that applies

If you are unsure which items to include, ask the supplier for a feasibility review before you finalise the drawing.

## Material and heat treatment choices

Bogie parts are usually made from carbon or alloy steel, chosen for the load and the section size. The grades listed for Varsha Forgings include EN8, EN9, EN19, EN24, EN31, 42CrMo4 and AISI 4140, among others. Your design authority sets the grade, and the supplier confirms that it can be forged and heat treated to reach the properties you need.

Heat treatment sets the final strength. Ask which treatments the supplier performs in-house, how furnace temperature is recorded, and how hardness is checked on each batch.

## Traceability for rail components

Rail customers expect full traceability, so ask how the supplier links each forging to its steel heat. A sound system gives each raw-material heat a batch code, marks that code on the part, and carries the code and heat number onto the inspection reports.

Ask also for the material certificate type. EN 10204 type 3.1 is common, and some customers request type 3.2 with third-party validation.

## Testing and inspection to ask for

For parts under repeated load, agree the test plan early:

- Chemistry check of incoming steel
- Hardness testing on each heat-treatment batch
- Magnetic particle inspection for surface cracks
- Ultrasonic testing where the drawing requires it
- Dimensional inspection against the drawing

Agree which checks apply to every part and which to samples. Confirm how nonconforming parts are held and recorded.

## Planning samples and first supply

Railway programmes tend to run on long cycles, so plan the early stages with care. Start with a feasibility review on one drawing, and agree the tolerances, the machining allowance and the inspection method before dies are made. Ask for first samples with their full inspection reports, and compare them with the drawing yourself or through your inspection agency.

Once the sample is approved, agree the supply schedule and the marking method in writing. Clear records at this stage make later audits much easier.

## Approval is a separate question

Indian Railways applies its own approval system to suppliers of railway components. If your programme requires that approval, confirm it in writing before you place an order. Our RDSO checklist covers this step in detail, so this guide does not repeat it.

Varsha Forgings is an RDSO approved hot closed-die forging company, operating since 1989 from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), India. The company also holds IATF 16949 and supports PPAP at Levels 1 to 5.
