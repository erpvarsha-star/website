### H2: Forgings for Gears, Shafts and Driveline Parts

Transmissions and drivelines turn engine power into motion. Their parts carry high torque, run in contact with each other, and must survive millions of load cycles. A gear tooth sees bending at its root and contact stress on its flank. A shaft sees torsion and fatigue. For these parts, designers often choose forgings, because hot forging aligns the grain with the shape of the part and gives a dense, consistent structure for the heat treatment and machining that follow.

Varsha Forgings Private Limited has made hot closed-die forgings since 1989 at Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad). We forge in carbon and alloy steel, from 1 kg to 25 kg, to your drawing. Our driveline range is for conventional engine and driveline applications. It does not include EV motor parts, EV gearbox or EV powertrain parts, or EV gear blanks.

### H2: Transmission and Driveline Parts We Make

**Forged:** gear blanks, carrier gear blanks, lay shaft gear blanks, pinions, shaft joints, yokes, pawl park, gear shifter levers, reverse gears, clutch bodies and differential gear blanks, with camshafts, axle shafts, crankshafts and connecting rods for engine and axle applications.

**Machined:** planet gears, helical gears, crown wheel and pinion sets.

A gear blank is the forged and heat-treated starting shape before teeth are cut. Supplying a consistent blank matters because any variation in hardness or structure shows up later in the finished gear.

### H2: Grades and Heat Treatment for Gears and Shafts

Gears are normally made in case-hardening steel: a low-carbon alloy that is carburized to give a hard surface over a tough core. Through-hardening alloy steel suits shafts and yokes that need strength throughout the section. We forge in 20MnCr5, 16MnCr5, 20MoCr4E and SAE8620, and in 42CrMo4, EN19, EN24 and AISI 4140. See our steel equivalency matrix for how these grades line up between DIN/EN, SAE/AISI and BS.

We offer vacuum carburizing, induction hardening, double quench and temper, normalizing and stress relieving. Heat treatment is checked by hardness testing (Brinell, Rockwell and micro-Vickers) and metallurgical examination, including grain size to ASTM E112. Magnetic particle inspection and ultrasonic testing are available for crack and internal defect checks, and we carry out 100% magnetic particle inspection on safety-critical parts.

### H2: Forge, Heat-Treat, Machine

Billets are cut on CNC circular saws, heated by induction between 1,100 and 1,250 degrees C, and forged hot in closed dies on presses from 800 T to 2,500 T or on friction drop hammers of 1 to 3 MT. Our die shop works in H13 and DIN 1.2714 tool steel, and we simulate metal flow in SimuFact Forming before cutting a die.

After forging come heat treatment, shot blasting, grinding and deburring, crack testing and machining. Grinding is done in-house. Machining includes facing and centering, CNC and VMC work, drilling and gear hobbing, extended by a dedicated base of machining vendors. Forging tolerance is plus or minus 0.5 mm. Machining tolerance is plus or minus 0.02 mm.

### H2: Quality and Traceability

We hold IATF 16949, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, and CQI-9 for heat treatment. PPAP submission is available at Levels 1 to 5, with APQP and IMDS. Statistical process control runs on key characteristics at a capability index of 1.67 or better.

Every batch carries a code per raw-material heat, and the material test report and the pre-delivery inspection report carry the heat number and traceability code. Material certificates are EN 10204 type 3.1, and 3.2 on request.
