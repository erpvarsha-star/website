### H2: What Electric Mobility Changes for Forged Parts

General engineering background, not a claim about any one supplier. An electric vehicle usually carries a heavy battery pack low in the chassis. That adds mass the suspension must carry, and instant motor torque changes how loads reach the wheels. Without an engine noise to mask them, vibration and road noise also become more noticeable to the driver. Suspension and steering parts therefore face loads similar in kind to those of conventional vehicles, often larger in size, and in a quiet vehicle any noise, vibration or harshness is easier to notice.

Forgings suit such parts. Hot closed-die forging aligns the grain with the part, giving good strength and fatigue life in a compact section, which helps where space and mass are both limited.

### H2: What Varsha Forgings Supplies

Our supply for electric vehicles is suspension and steering parts. We record supply for electric vehicles as a milestone in 2024, and we also serve electric transit buses.

The part families we forge for suspension and steering, from 1 kg to 25 kg, include:

- Ball joints, drag links and tie rods
- Anti-roll bar linkages
- Axle mounts and swing-arm mounts
- Steering levers
- Torque rods, radius rods and V-links

We make tie rods as complete assemblies, and also forge tie rod ends alone for buyers who want only the forging.

### H2: What We Do Not Supply

To be clear about scope: we do not supply EV motor shafts, rotor shafts, EV gearbox or powertrain parts, or EV gear blanks. Our electric mobility work is suspension and steering. If your enquiry is for motor or gearbox components, we will tell you at the feasibility stage that it is outside our range, and that early answer is meant to save your time.

### H2: How We Develop a New EV Part

We start from your drawing, in 2D or 3D. Feasibility review comes before quotation, and we return a feasibility response within 48 hours for a single drawing or part. We simulate metal flow in SimuFact Forming before cutting dies, forge hot in closed dies on presses from 800 T to 2,500 T or on friction drop hammers, and heat-treat to your specification. Forging tolerance is plus or minus 0.5 mm and machining tolerance plus or minus 0.02 mm.

We accept prototype and low-volume enquiries alongside series production, which suits EV programmes where design changes are frequent and volumes are still forming. Minimum order quantity is project-dependent and is discussed at enquiry.

### H2: Materials and Heat Treatment

We forge carbon and alloy steels only, with no stainless grades. Our grade list includes EN19, EN24, 42CrMo4, AISI 4140, 20MnCr5 and SAE8620, among others, and the grade on your drawing governs. Heat treatment options include induction hardening, double quench and temper, normalizing and stress relieving, checked by hardness testing and grain-size examination to ASTM E112. Our steel equivalency matrix shows how these grades line up between DIN/EN, SAE/AISI and BS.

### H2: Quality for Safety-Critical Parts

Steering and suspension parts are safety-critical, and electric vehicles are no different. We hold IATF 16949, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, and CQI-9 for heat treatment. PPAP submission is available at Levels 1 to 5, with APQP and IMDS. We run 100% magnetic particle inspection on safety-critical parts and statistical process control at a capability index of 1.67 or better. Every batch carries a code per raw-material heat, with EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificates, and 3.2 on request.
