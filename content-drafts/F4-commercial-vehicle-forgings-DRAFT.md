## Page copy

### H2: Forgings for Trucks, Buses and Heavy-Duty Vehicles

A commercial vehicle works harder than a passenger car. It carries heavy loads over long distances, and the parts that steer, suspend and drive it see repeated shock and fatigue loading. For these parts, designers often specify forgings. Hot closed-die forging shapes heated steel in a die, so the grain flow follows the part, which helps fatigue life in highly loaded sections.

Varsha Forgings Private Limited has made hot closed-die forgings since 1989 at Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad). Commercial vehicles are one of the industries we serve, with steering, suspension and driveline parts. Over 60% of our production serves OEMs, and we work as both a Tier 1 and a Tier 2 supplier. Our OEM customers include Daimler, Ashok Leyland, MAN Trucks India, Mahindra Trucks & Buses, Paccar, Scania and Tata Daewoo.

### H2: Commercial Vehicle Parts We Forge

We forge to your drawing, from 1 kg to 25 kg, in carbon and alloy steel. The part families below are those our commercial-vehicle customers draw from.

- **Steering:** idler arm, sector shaft, drop arm, steering lever, end cover, worm, spring saddle, drag links, ball joints, tie rods and tie rod ends, steering knuckles
- **Suspension:** eye end, spherical ball pin, swing arm, anti-roll bar linkages, axle mounts, swing-arm mounts, torque rods, radius rods and V-links
- **Driveline and engine:** camshafts, axle shafts, crankshafts, connecting rods, yokes, shaft joints, reverse gears, clutch bodies, differential gear blanks
- **Other:** front wheel hub, wheel carrier, tow hooks, bearing bushes, end-body turning

We make tie rods as complete assemblies, and we also forge tie rod ends alone for buyers who want only the forging. Rubber-bonded parts such as engine mounts and vibration isolators are made through a dedicated rubber-bonding partner, under our quality control.

### H2: Recognised by Commercial Vehicle OEMs

Our milestone record includes the Daimler India Best Supplier Award in 2016, a Daimler India Performance Appreciation Letter in 2023, and the Ashok Leyland Medal in 2023.

### H2: Quality Built Around Safety-Critical Parts

Steering and suspension parts carry safety responsibility, so our quality system is built around them. We hold IATF 16949, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, and CQI-9 for heat treatment. All safety-critical parts used in our assemblies have been ARAI certified.

On the shop floor we run statistical process control on key characteristics, at a capability index of 1.67 or better, and carry out 100% magnetic particle inspection on safety-critical parts. Hardness is tested by Brinell, Rockwell and micro-Vickers, grain size to ASTM E112, and we use ultrasonic testing. Our standards room has a Faro Arm CMM, a coordinate measuring machine, a profile projector and a contour tester. Each batch carries a code per raw-material heat, so a part can be traced to its steel. Material certificates are EN 10204 type 3.1, and 3.2 on request. We support PPAP submission at Levels 1 to 5, with APQP and IMDS.

### H2: From Forging to Assembly

Our forging plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) runs presses from 800 T to 2,500 T and friction drop hammers of 1 to 3 MT. Heat treatment, grinding and in-house machining follow forging, with a base of machining vendors to extend capacity. Forging tolerance is plus or minus 0.5 mm and machining tolerance plus or minus 0.02 mm.

Our Pune plant carries out assembly: hydraulic presses, automated circlip application, bolt torque control with a 100% torque audit, and pressure-decay leak testing. It supplies customers in the Chakan, Talegaon and Bhosari belt on a just-in-time and Kanban basis.

### H2: Electric Buses and Electric Vehicles

We also serve electric transit buses. For electric vehicles our supply is suspension and steering parts. We do not supply EV motor shafts or EV gearbox and powertrain parts.
