## H2: Steering Linkage Forgings Made by Varsha Forgings

A tie rod end connects the steering rack or drag link to the steering knuckle. It carries every steering input and every road shock, and it must not fail. That is why buyers specify forged parts for this job: hot closed-die forging aligns the grain flow of the steel with the shape of the part, which gives better fatigue life than a casting of the same size.

Varsha Forgings Private Limited has made hot closed-die forgings since 1989. We supply complete tie rod assemblies, and we also forge tie rod ends on their own for buyers who need only the forging. Our steering and suspension range also includes drag links, ball joints, steering levers, steering knuckles, idler arms, drop arms, anti-roll bar linkages, torque rods, radius rods and V-links. We forge components from 1 kg to 25 kg at our plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad).

## H2: Grades We Forge

We forge carbon and alloy steels only. Every grade below is on our current forging list. We confirm the grade for your part during feasibility review.

| Grade | Type | Why designers choose it |
|---|---|---|
| EN8 | Medium carbon steel | Economical, good strength after quench and temper |
| EN9 | Medium carbon steel | Higher carbon content than EN8 for greater strength |
| EN19 | Chromium-molybdenum alloy steel | Good strength and toughness after heat treatment |
| EN24 | Nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy steel | High strength and toughness for heavily loaded parts |
| 42CrMo4 | Chromium-molybdenum alloy steel | Widely used European-standard grade for safety parts |
| AISI 4140 | Chromium-molybdenum alloy steel | US-standard equivalent family to 42CrMo4 |
| SAE1541 | Medium carbon, manganese steel | Strength from manganese; common in forged axle and steering parts |

The last column is general engineering guidance. We do not forge stainless steel.

## H2: Process and In-House Quality Control

Every tie rod forging follows one controlled route: material inspection, saw cutting, induction billet heating at 1,100–1,250 °C, forging on presses or hammers, inspection, heat treatment, hardness testing, shot blasting, grinding and deburring, crack testing, machining and final inspection.

- **Forging:** presses from 800 T to 2,500 T and friction drop hammers from 1 to 3 MT. Dies are made in our own die shop from H13 and DIN 1.2714 die steel, and die filling is simulated in SimuFact Forming before we cut steel.
- **Heat treatment:** double quench and tempering, normalizing, stress relieving, induction hardening and vacuum carburizing, all offered.
- **Testing:** Brinell, Rockwell and micro-Vickers hardness, ASTM E112 grain size, ultrasonic testing and a vacuum emission spectrometer for chemistry.
- **Crack detection:** 100% magnetic particle inspection (MPI) on safety-critical parts, which includes steering linkage.
- **Measurement:** Faro Arm CMM, profile projector and contour tester in a dedicated standards room. Forging tolerance is ±0.5 mm and machining tolerance is ±0.02 mm.
- **Statistical control:** SPC on key control characteristics, with a Cpk of 1.67 or better.

## H2: Traceability, PPAP and Documentation

Each raw-material heat receives a batch code. On ball studs and ball pins the code is engraved on the forging die, and on sockets and housings it is surface-marked. Setup and in-process reports carry the heat number. The material test report and the PDIR carry the heat number, traceability code and invoice number, so a field problem can be traced back to one heat of steel.

Our documentation suits OEM and Tier 1 purchasing:

- IATF 16949, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified
- PPAP submission at Levels 1–5, with APQP and IMDS
- EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificates, and type 3.2 on request

## H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a tie rod end forging?**
It is the forged body of the joint that links the steering linkage to the steering knuckle. Hot closed-die forging presses heated steel into a die so the grain follows the part's shape, which supports fatigue strength in a part that sees constant load reversals.

**Which steel grades does Varsha Forgings use for steering linkage forgings?**
We forge EN8, EN9, EN19, EN24, 42CrMo4, AISI 4140, SAE1541 and other carbon and alloy grades on our current list. We do not forge stainless steel. The final grade depends on your drawing and strength class.

**What weight range and tolerances can you supply?**
Forged components from 1 kg to 25 kg. Forging tolerance is ±0.5 mm and machining tolerance is ±0.02 mm.

**What inspection is done on safety-critical steering forgings?**
100% magnetic particle inspection on safety-critical parts, plus hardness testing, grain size checks to ASTM E112, ultrasonic testing and dimensional checks on CMM and profile projector.

**Do you offer PPAP and material certificates?**
Yes. PPAP submission at Levels 1–5, with APQP and IMDS. Material certificates are EN 10204 type 3.1, and type 3.2 on request.

**Can I order a prototype or a small batch?**
Yes. Prototype and low-volume enquiries are accepted alongside series production. Send a drawing and we respond on feasibility within 48 hours for a single part.
