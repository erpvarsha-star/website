## H2: Railway Forgings from an RDSO-Approved Indian Forge

Varsha Forgings Private Limited is an RDSO-approved supplier to Indian Railways. We have made hot closed-die forgings since 1989 at our plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), with forgings from 1 kg to 25 kg, machined to ±0.02 mm where the drawing calls for it.

Railway buyers carry two risks that general forging buyers do not: a part that fails on a train has serious consequences, and every supplier must be on an approved list before a purchase order can be raised. We hold the approval, we also hold IATF 16949 and ISO 9001:2015, and we run one documented quality system for forging, heat treatment, machining and inspection.

## H2: What RDSO Approval Means for a Buyer

RDSO is the Research Designs and Standards Organisation, the technical body of Indian Railways under the Ministry of Railways, based in Lucknow. It writes specifications and drawings for railway components and approves the manufacturers that may supply them.

In general terms, approval means:

- **The supplier was assessed.** RDSO or its inspecting agency reviews the plant, process control, testing facilities and records before a firm is listed.
- **Approval is specific.** A firm is typically approved for named items to a named specification and drawing. A buyer should always check that the item being purchased is covered.
- **The list is the gate.** Railway tenders and procurement normally require the supplier, or the source, to be on the approved list for the item.
- **Approval is not a lifetime pass.** Approvals are reviewed, so a buyer should ask for current documentation.

This section is general information about the Indian system, not a statement of our approval scope. Send your drawing or RDSO specification number with your enquiry and we confirm during feasibility review what we can supply against it.

## H2: Forging, Heat Treatment and Testing

We forge carbon and alloy steels on presses from 800 T to 2,500 T and friction drop hammers from 1 to 3 MT, after induction billet heating at 1,100–1,250 °C. Dies come from our own die shop. Heat treatment options include normalizing, double quench and tempering, stress relieving and induction hardening, followed by shot blasting and in-house grinding.

For testing, our metallurgy laboratory runs a vacuum emission spectrometer for chemistry, Brinell, Rockwell and micro-Vickers hardness testing, ASTM E112 grain size analysis, a microscope with image analyser and ultrasonic testing. Safety-critical parts receive 100% magnetic particle inspection. Dimensions are checked on a Faro Arm CMM, a profile projector and a contour tester, and forging tolerance is ±0.5 mm.

## H2: Traceability and Documentation

Each raw-material heat is given a batch code, and the setup and in-process reports carry the heat number. The material test report and PDIR carry the heat number, traceability code and invoice number. Material certificates are issued to EN 10204 type 3.1, with type 3.2 on request. For customers who need it, we also support PPAP submission at Levels 1–5, APQP and IMDS.

Where a railway assembly needs rubber-bonded parts, such as bonded housings or vibration isolators, the bonding is done through a dedicated rubber-bonding partner under Varsha's quality control.
