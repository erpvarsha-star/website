## H2: Closed-Die Forgings Built to European Specifications

Our forging presses run from 800 T to 2,500 T, supported by friction drop hammers from 1 to 3 MT, an in-house die shop, heat treatment furnaces and a metallurgy laboratory with vacuum emission spectrometry. We forge carbon and alloy steels including EN8, EN9, EN19, EN24, 20MnCr5, 16MnCr5, 42CrMo4, SAE8620, AISI 4140 and ASTM A105, LF2, F11 and F22 grades.

Typical components for European customers include:

- Steering and suspension forgings: tie rods, drag links, ball joints, steering knuckles, torque rods, radius rods and V-links
- Transmission and driveline: gear blanks, shafts, yokes, connecting rods and crown wheel & pinion sets
- Railway components: bogie guide links, traction rods and brake levers — we are an RDSO-approved supplier to Indian Railways
- Oil & gas and off-highway: flanges, valve bodies and linkage components

Forging tolerances of ±0.5 mm and machining tolerances of ±0.02 mm are verified with Faro Arm CMM, profile projectors and contour measurement in our standards room. Safety-critical parts receive 100% magnetic particle inspection.
