## H2: Steering Knuckle Forgings Made by Varsha Forgings

A steering knuckle holds the wheel hub and bearing, and it connects the suspension arms and the steering linkage to the wheel. It carries braking, cornering and steering loads at the same time, so a failure is a safety event. For that reason buyers specify a forged knuckle. Hot closed-die forging presses heated steel into a die so the grain flow follows the shape of the part, which supports fatigue strength in a component that sees constant load reversals.

Varsha Forgings Private Limited has made hot closed-die forgings since 1989 at our plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad). Steering knuckles are part of our engine, driveline and steering range, and we forge components from 1 kg to 25 kg. Over 60% of our production serves OEMs, as a Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier.

## H2: The Steering Range Around the Knuckle

A knuckle works with other parts, and many buyers source them together. Varsha forges and machines a wide steering and suspension range:

- **Steering gear parts:** idler arm, sector shaft, drop arm, steering levers, end cover, worm and spring saddle
- **Linkage:** tie rods (complete assemblies, and tie rod ends as forgings only), drag links, ball joints and anti-roll bar linkages
- **Suspension:** upper control arm, swing arms, torque rods, radius rods, V-links, axle mounts and spherical ball pins
- **Wheel end:** front wheel hub and wheel carrier

Sourcing several parts from one forge keeps documentation, PPAP and traceability under one quality system.

## H2: Process and In-House Quality Control

Every knuckle forging follows one controlled route: material inspection, saw cutting, induction billet heating at 1,100–1,250 °C, forging on presses or hammers, inspection, heat treatment, hardness testing, shot blasting, grinding and deburring, crack testing, machining and final inspection.

- **Forging:** presses from 800 T to 2,500 T and friction drop hammers from 1 to 3 MT. Dies are made in our own die shop from H13 and DIN 1.2714 die steel, and die filling is simulated in SimuFact Forming before we cut steel.
- **Heat treatment:** double quench and tempering, normalizing, stress relieving, induction hardening and vacuum carburizing, all offered.
- **Testing:** Brinell, Rockwell and micro-Vickers hardness, ASTM E112 grain size, ultrasonic testing and a vacuum emission spectrometer for chemistry.
- **Crack detection:** 100% magnetic particle inspection (MPI) on safety-critical parts, which includes steering parts.
- **Measurement:** Faro Arm CMM, profile projector and contour tester in a dedicated standards room. Forging tolerance is ±0.5 mm and machining tolerance is ±0.02 mm.
- **Statistical control:** SPC on key control characteristics, with a Cpk of 1.67 or better.
- **Machining:** grinding is done in-house, and a dedicated vendor base extends machining capacity under our quality control.

## H2: Traceability, PPAP and Documentation

Each raw-material heat receives a batch code. On some parts the code is engraved on the forging die, and on others it is surface-marked. Setup and in-process reports carry the heat number. The material test report and the PDIR carry the heat number, traceability code and invoice number, so a field problem can be traced back to one heat of steel.

Our documentation suits OEM and Tier 1 purchasing:

- IATF 16949, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified
- PPAP submission at Levels 1–5, with APQP and IMDS
- EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificates, and type 3.2 on request
