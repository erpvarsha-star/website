## H2: Prototype and Low-Volume Forging Enquiries Welcome

Many buyers testing a new supplier, a new part or a new market need a small first order, not a production contract. Varsha Forgings Private Limited accepts prototype and low-volume enquiries alongside series production. You do not need a large programme to start a conversation.

We have made hot closed-die forgings since 1989 at our plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad). We forge components from 1 kg to 25 kg in carbon and alloy steel. The same quality system, inspection routes and documentation that apply to our series parts apply to the parts you send us for evaluation, because a prototype is only useful if it behaves like the production part.

## H2: Who Uses Prototype and Low-Volume Forgings

- **New product development teams** who need forged parts for validation before committing to production tooling
- **Buyers qualifying a new supplier** who want to inspect real parts and documents before placing a series order
- **Overseas buyers testing India sourcing** who want to place a first, limited order and review quality and communication
- **Spare and replacement part buyers** whose volumes are modest but whose quality requirements are not

We supply commercial vehicles, agriculture, railway, electric vehicles (suspension and steering parts only), mining and off-highway, construction, infrastructure, defense and oil and gas customers. Over 60% of our production serves OEMs as a Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier.

## H2: How the Process Works

Our RFQ sequence is the same for a prototype and for a series part:

1. **Customer drawing.** Send a 2D or 3D drawing to marketing@varshaforgings.com, or call +91 8411000551.
2. **Feasibility review.** We respond within 48 hours for a single drawing or part. We check shape, weight, grade and tolerances against what hot closed-die forging can deliver.
3. **Quotation.** You receive a quotation for the quantity you need.
4. **PPAP.** Where your programme needs it, we plan PPAP submission at Levels 1–5, with APQP and IMDS.

The 48-hour feasibility response applies to a single drawing or part. For a package of several parts, we will tell you the review time when we receive the files.

## H2: What You Get with a Small Order

A small quantity does not mean a smaller quality standard. Parts follow the same controlled route as series production: material inspection, saw cutting, induction billet heating at 1,100–1,250 °C, forging, inspection, heat treatment, hardness testing, shot blasting, grinding and deburring, crack testing, machining and final inspection.

- **Traceability:** each raw-material heat receives a batch code, and the material test report carries the heat number and traceability code.
- **Certificates:** EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificates, and type 3.2 on request.
- **Inspection:** hardness testing (Brinell, Rockwell, micro-Vickers), ASTM E112 grain size, ultrasonic testing, and 100% magnetic particle inspection on safety-critical parts.
- **Measurement:** Faro Arm CMM, profile projector and contour tester in a dedicated standards room.
- **Tolerances:** forging ±0.5 mm and machining ±0.02 mm.
- **Simulation:** die filling is simulated in SimuFact Forming before steel is cut, which helps catch fill problems early.

A prototype is often the first step. If the part is approved, the same drawing, grade and inspection plan carry into series production at our plants. Our OEM customers include Daimler, Ashok Leyland, MAN Trucks India, Mahindra Trucks & Buses, Paccar, Scania and Tata Daewoo. Samples and urgent needs can move by air freight. Terms are Letter of Credit or advance payment. Virtual plant tours are available, and customers are welcome to visit the plants after an RFQ.
