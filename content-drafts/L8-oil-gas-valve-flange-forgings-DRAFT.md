## H2: Oil and Gas Forgings Made by Varsha Forgings

Valve bodies and flanges hold pressure, and many sit in lines carrying hydrocarbons, steam or process fluids. Buyers choose forged bodies and flanges because hot closed-die forging produces a dense, directional grain structure in the steel, which supports strength and integrity in pressure-containing parts. In this industry the steel grade is the first decision, and the buyer's specification drives it.

Varsha Forgings Private Limited has made hot closed-die forgings since 1989 at our plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad). Oil and gas and petrochemical is one of the industries we serve, and flanges and valve bodies are part of our range. We forge components from 1 kg to 25 kg in carbon and alloy steel.

## H2: Grades We Forge: A105, LF2, F11 and F22

Buyers in this sector search by grade, so here is how the four common ones differ. The descriptions below are general engineering guidance. Your drawing and purchase specification decide the grade, and we confirm it during feasibility review.

| Grade | Type | Typically chosen for |
|---|---|---|
| ASTM A105 | Carbon steel | General-purpose flanges, fittings and valve parts at ambient and moderately elevated temperature |
| LF2 | Low-temperature carbon steel | Flanges and valve parts in cold-service lines |
| F11 | Chromium-molybdenum alloy steel | Flanges and valve parts in high-temperature service |
| F22 | Chromium-molybdenum alloy steel | Higher-temperature, higher-strength service than F11, including steam lines |

We forge carbon and alloy steels. Our current list also includes EN8, EN9, EN19, EN24, 20MnCr5, 16MnCr5, 42CrMo4, 20MoCr4E, SAE1541, SAE8620, AISI 4140 and AISI 1045. We offer normalizing, double quench and tempering and stress relieving, and the heat treatment route for your grade is agreed against your specification.

## H2: Flanges and Valve Bodies from India

Flanges and valve bodies are part of our confirmed product range for oil and gas. We forge to your drawing, within 1 kg to 25 kg. Forging tolerance is ±0.5 mm and machining tolerance is ±0.02 mm. A dedicated vendor base extends our machining capacity under our quality control, and grinding is done in-house.

Send us the drawing, the grade and the specification it must meet. If you also need related parts from the same forge, our range includes steering, suspension, driveline and railway components, which share the same quality system and documentation.

## H2: Process and In-House Quality Control

Every forging follows one controlled route: material inspection, saw cutting, induction billet heating at 1,100–1,250 °C, forging on presses or hammers, inspection, heat treatment, hardness testing, shot blasting, grinding and deburring, crack testing, machining and final inspection.

- **Forging:** presses from 800 T to 2,500 T and friction drop hammers from 1 to 3 MT. Dies are made in our own die shop from H13 and DIN 1.2714 die steel, and die filling is simulated in SimuFact Forming.
- **Chemistry:** vacuum emission spectrometer in our metallurgy lab, with microscope and image analyser.
- **Testing:** Brinell, Rockwell and micro-Vickers hardness, ASTM E112 grain size and ultrasonic testing.
- **Crack detection:** magnetic particle inspection, with 100% MPI on safety-critical parts.
- **Measurement:** Faro Arm CMM, profile projector and contour tester in a dedicated standards room.
- **Statistical control:** SPC on key control characteristics, with a Cpk of 1.67 or better.

Any further test your specification requires should be stated in your RFQ, and we will confirm during feasibility review whether and how we can meet it.

## H2: Traceability and Documentation

Each raw-material heat receives a batch code, and setup and in-process reports carry the heat number. The material test report and the PDIR carry the heat number, traceability code and invoice number, so each forging traces back to one heat of steel. This matters in pressure-containing parts, where the buyer needs to prove the material.

- EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificates, and type 3.2 on request
- IATF 16949, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified
- PPAP submission at Levels 1–5, with APQP and IMDS where your programme requires it

Raw material is sourced from tier-one steel mills.
