### Body copy

**H1: Automotive Forgings Supplier Hub**

Varsha Forgings is an IATF 16949 certified automotive forgings supplier based in Maharashtra, India, operating since 1989. We run the full program from one supplier: design review, in-house tooling, closed-die forging, machining, inspection and assembly — so OEM and Tier 1 buyers don't have to coordinate separate vendors for each step.

**What a full-program supplier removes from your plate**

- **Design review** — we review your drawing for forgeability before tooling is cut, catching issues that would otherwise surface as scrap or rework later.
- **In-house tooling** — forging dies are designed and manufactured in-house, so tooling lead time and revisions stay under our control, not a third party's.
- **Forging** — closed-die forging on presses and hammers ranging from 500T to 2,500T capacity, producing components with the grain flow and dimensional accuracy automotive programs require.
- **Machining** — in-house CNC turning, VMC and gear hobbing take the forging to finished print dimensions without handing off to a separate machine shop.
- **Inspection and traceability** — PPAP Level 3 submission capability, with batch-level traceability from raw material heat number through final dispatch.
- **Assembly** — hardware pressing, sleeve insertion, and other sub-assembly operations completed before the part ships.

**Certifications governing every program**

Production runs under IATF 16949, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018 and ISO 14001:2015 certification, with PPAP Level 3 submission capability for new part approvals.

**Track record**

Varsha Forgings holds a design patent granted by the Indian Government (2011), won the India SME Excellence Award in Innovation & Invention (2014), and was named a final winner in the 11th Edition of the India SME 100 Awards (2025). The company has supplied OEMs including Daimler, Ashok Leyland, MAN Trucks India, Mahindra Trucks & Buses, Paccar, Scania and Tata Daewoo, and exports to customers in Germany, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Austria and the US.

## `/tool-manufactring` — Tool Manufacturing

### SEO title
In-House Forging Die & Tool Manufacturing | Varsha Forgings

### Meta description
Varsha Forgings manufactures its own forging dies and tools in-house using VMC, laser etching, radial drilling and Dell Cam 3D programming — faster tooling turnaround and tighter die control for every forging program.

### Body copy

**H1: In-House Tool and Die Manufacturing**

Varsha Forgings designs and manufactures its own forging dies and tooling in-house, rather than outsourcing die work to a third-party tool room. That keeps tooling lead time, revision turnaround and die quality under our own control for every program we quote.

**Equipment and process**

- **VMC (5 units)** — vertical machining centers cut die cavities to the tolerances closed-die forging requires.
- **Laser etching** — used for marking and fine detail work on die surfaces.
- **Radial drilling** — supports the drilling operations die blocks need before and after cavity machining.
- **Dell Cam, 3D programming software** — die geometry is programmed in 3D before it's cut, reducing trial-and-error on the shop floor.

**Why in-house tooling matters to OEM buyers**

- **Faster turnaround** — a die revision doesn't wait in another company's queue.
- **Custom tooling** — dies are built to the specific part geometry a customer's drawing calls for, not adapted from a catalogue tool.
- **Consistent quality control** — the team that designs the die also machines it, closing the loop between design intent and the physical tool.
- **Reduced lead times** — tooling and forging are coordinated under one roof at the Aurangabad facility, established 1989.
