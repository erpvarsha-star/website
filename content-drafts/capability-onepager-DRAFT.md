## Varsha Forgings Private Limited — Capability Sheet

**Positioning line (use everywhere):**
Varsha Forgings — closed-die forgings, precision machining and assembly from one Indian supplier, 1–25 kg, IATF 16949, PPAP L3, since 1989.

**Who we are**
Varsha Forgings Private Limited is an Indian Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier of hot closed-die forgings, precision machining and sub-assembly, serving automotive, agriculture, railway, electric vehicle, oil & gas/petrochemical, mining & off-highway, electric transit bus, and construction/infrastructure/defense OEMs. Over 60% of production serves OEMs directly. Founded 1989 in Aurangabad (Forging Division); the Pune plant (Markal, Khed — precision machining and assembly) has operated since 2007.

**Two plants, one supply chain**
- **Aurangabad (MIDC Waluj), established 1989** — hot closed-die forging. Presses 800 T–2,500 T, friction drop hammers 1–3 MT, in-house die shop, heat treatment, metallurgical laboratory.
- **Pune (Markal, Khed), operating since 2007** — precision CNC/VMC machining, gear hobbing, assembly, extended by a dedicated base of machining vendors. JIT/Kanban supply to the Chakan–Talegaon–Bhosari OEM belt.

**Capability ranges**
- Forged component weight: 1 kg – 25 kg
- Annual capacity: over 12,000 metric tons per annum; over 3 million assorted pieces per year
- Tolerances: forging ±0.5 mm, machining ±0.02 mm
- Feasibility response: within 48 hours for a single drawing/part

**Materials**
Carbon and alloy steels: EN8, EN9, EN19, EN24, EN31, 20MnCr5, 16MnCr5, 42CrMo4, 20MoCr4E, SAE1541, SAE8620, AISI 4140, AISI 1045, ASTM A105, LF2, F11, F22. (No stainless steel.)

**Processes**
Hot closed-die forging only (no warm, cold or open-die forging). Surface/heat treatments: vacuum carburizing, induction hardening, hot-dip galvanizing, double quench & temper, stress relieving, normalizing. In-house grinding. Process flow: material inspection → billet heating → forging → heat treatment → hardness testing → shot blasting → grinding & deburring → crack testing → machining → painting → final inspection.

**Quality**
IATF 16949 · ISO 9001:2015 · ISO 45001:2018 · ISO 14001:2015 · PPAP submission Levels 1–5 · APQP · IMDS · RDSO approved (Indian Railways). SPC with Cpk ≥ 1.67. Testing: Brinell/Rockwell/micro-Vickers hardness, ASTM E112 grain size, ultrasonic testing, 100% MPI on safety-critical parts. Material certificates: EN 10204 type 3.1, 3.2 on request.

**Products**
Forged and machined components for steering, suspension, transmission/driveline, engine, and axle systems — including ball joints, drag links, tie rods, torque rods, radius rods, V-links, steering knuckles, crankshafts, connecting rods, differential gear blanks, camshafts, axle shafts, control-rod assemblies. Rubber-bonded assemblies (bonding via a dedicated partner, under Varsha's quality control): engine mounts, vibration isolators, bonded housings. Railway: bogie guide links, traction rods, brake levers, drawbar hooks, damper brackets. EV: suspension and steering components only.

**Customers**
Daimler, Ashok Leyland, MAN Trucks India, Mahindra Trucks & Buses, Paccar, Scania, Tata Daewoo, Tata Motors, Bajaj, TVS, John Deere, TAFE, Force Motors, VE Commercial Vehicles, and others.

Markets: Germany, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Austria, United States, Asia-Pacific, Middle East & Africa. ISPM-15 export crates, GPS-tracked containers, JNPT (Nhava Sheva) port access, air freight for samples, drawings accepted in 2D and 3D, payment by Letter of Credit or advance.
