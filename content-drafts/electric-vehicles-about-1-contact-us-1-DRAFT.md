## /about-1 (Closed Die Forging)

### SEO title
About Closed-Die Forging | Varsha Forgings | Process & Capabilities

### Meta description
Closed-die forging explained: process overview, press capacity, materials, tolerances and comparison with other forming methods. Varsha Forgings, Aurangabad India.

### Body copy

**H1: What Is Closed-Die Forging?**

Closed-die forging shapes heated metal between two dies that fully enclose the workpiece, forcing the material under pressure to fill the die cavity and take its exact shape. The result is a component with a continuous internal grain structure that follows the part's contours, giving it higher strength and better dimensional accuracy than a casting of the same shape. Varsha Forgings has operated its forging division from MIDC Waluj, Aurangabad since 1989.

**H2: What press capacity does Varsha Forgings use?**

Varsha Forgings operates closed-die presses and hammers ranging from 500T to 2,500T capacity. Press selection depends on the component's size, material and complexity: smaller, simpler geometries run on lower-tonnage lines, while larger or more intricate forgings need the higher press force to fully fill the die in a single heat.

**H2: How does closed-die forging compare to other methods?**

Closed-die forging sits between open-die forging and casting in terms of shape complexity versus material properties. Open-die forging (hammering between flat or simple-shaped dies) is suited to large, simpler shapes and offers less dimensional control. Casting can produce complex geometries but introduces porosity and a less favourable grain structure, which matters for parts under cyclic or impact loading — axles, steering components, drivetrain parts. Closed-die forging is the standard where both precision and strength are required.

**H2: What materials and tolerances does Varsha Forgings work with?**

Varsha Forgings forges carbon and alloy steels to customer specification, and follows forging with in-house heat treatment, machining and inspection so the component arrives at final dimension and hardness in one supply chain rather than passing through separate vendors.

**FAQ**

**Q: What is closed-die forging?**
A: Closed-die forging shapes heated metal between two dies that enclose the workpiece, forcing metal to fill precise cavities and producing components with better grain flow, strength and dimensional accuracy than casting.

**Q: What press capacity does Varsha Forgings use for closed-die forging?**
A: Varsha Forgings operates closed-die presses and hammers ranging from 500T to 2,500T capacity.

**Q: How is closed-die forging different from open-die forging?**
A: Open-die forging uses simpler, flat or shaped dies suited to larger, less complex parts with less dimensional control; closed-die forging fully encloses the workpiece to produce precise, complex geometries.
