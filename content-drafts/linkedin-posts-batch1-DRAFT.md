## Post 4 — Thu, buyer education

**"Forging vs. casting" isn't just a materials question — it's a sourcing question.**

A forged part is worked under pressure, giving it a continuous grain structure and generally higher strength-to-weight for the same geometry — which is why safety-critical automotive and rail components are almost always specified as forgings, not castings.

If your drawing currently calls for a casting, ask your forging supplier what a closed-die equivalent would cost at your volume. It's often closer than you'd expect.
