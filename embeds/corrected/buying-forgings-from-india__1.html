Why India Offers Strong Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

1. Quality Failure Cost Avoidance

Varsha Forgings builds failure prevention into the process through:

  • Material verification by spectrometry, with a batch code per raw-material heat
  • Hardness testing after heat treatment
  • SPC on key control characteristics, Cpk ≥ 1.67
  • Magnetic particle inspection, 100% on safety-critical parts

Indian IATF 16949-certified manufacturers such as Varsha Forgings follow globally accepted automotive quality systems. Preventing even a single production line stoppage or warranty claim can outweigh apparent per-piece savings.
