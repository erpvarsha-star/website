Varsha Forgings supports EV range optimization through:

  • Forging simulation (SimuFact Forming 15.0) for metal-flow and die optimization.
  • CAD/FEA structural optimization to remove non-load-bearing mass from suspension linkages.
  • Micro-alloyed steel substitution replacing heavier cast iron or welded assemblies with high-strength forged alternatives.
  • Near-net-shape forging minimizing machining stock and material waste.
