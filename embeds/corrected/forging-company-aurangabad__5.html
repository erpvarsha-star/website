Engineering Design & Simulation

Engineering teams utilize Creo 3.0 for 3D CAD parametric design and SimuFact Forming 15.0 for finite element metal-flow simulation. This pre-production simulation predicts:

  • Grain flow patterns and potential forging defects (folds, underfill, cold shuts)
  • Die stress and thermal distribution
  • Material yield optimization

Die tooling is machined from hot-work tool steels AISI H13 and DIN 1.2714 (DB6), hardened and tempered to resist thermal shock and abrasive wear.
