The manufacturing footprint is intentionally divided to maximize metallurgical capability and precision engineering efficiency across the state.

1. Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), MIDC Waluj — The Heavy Forging Hub

Located in the heart of the Marathwada industrial belt, the Waluj facility is the center of metallurgical and heavy forging operations.

Positioned adjacent to India's largest automotive ecosystem, the Khed facility handles the precision finishing and assembly of forged components.
