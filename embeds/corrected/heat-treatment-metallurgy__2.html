Heat Treatment Processes & Specifications

1. Normalizing

Parameter Specification
Temperature850°C – 900°C (above upper critical temperature)
AtmosphereAir (still or forced)
Cooling RateModerate (air cool)
ResultUniform ferrite-pearlite microstructure, refined grain size
PurposeEliminate forging thermal stress, uniform grain size for machining or heat treatment
Typical ApplicationsMedium-carbon and alloy steel forgings prior to machining

2. Isothermal Annealing

Parameter Specification
Temperature850°C – 900°C, then rapid cool to 650°C – 700°C hold
AtmosphereControlled furnace
Cooling RateRapid transfer to isothermal hold
ResultUniform ferrite-pearlite structure, optimum hardness for machining
PurposeProduce machinable microstructure for gear blanks and CNC parts
Typical ApplicationsGear blank forgings prior to hobbing and high-alloy steel components

3. Quenching & Tempering (Hardening)

Parameter Specification
Austenitizing820°C – 880°C (depending on alloy)
Quench MediumOil, water or polymer quenchants
Tempering200°C – 650°C based on hardness target
ResultTempered martensite or lower bainite
PurposeHigh tensile strength and toughness balance
Typical ApplicationsTorque rods, radius rods, V-links, gear shafts, railway components

4. Stress Relieving

Parameter Specification
Temperature550°C – 650°C (below lower critical temperature)
AtmosphereControlled furnace
Cooling RateSlow furnace cool
ResultReduced residual stress and improved dimensional stability
PurposePrevent distortion during machining or service
Typical ApplicationsLarge forgings, welded assemblies and pre-machining stress relief

Decarburization Control & Measurement

Decarburization reduces surface hardness and fatigue resistance. Control measures include:

  • Controlled atmosphere furnaces where required
  • Minimum heating times
  • Rapid descaling after forging
Measurement Method Standard Acceptance
Micro-hardness TraverseCross-sectional sampleTotal decarb < 0.3 mm; Effective decarb < 0.1 mm
Metallographic ExaminationASTM E1077Ferrite layer depth measurement

Grain Flow Analysis

Metallographic sectioning and etching verify that grain flow follows component contours, providing superior fatigue strength compared to cast or bar-machined components.
