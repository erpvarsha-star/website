Assembly Operation Description
Hardware Pressing Insertion of pins, sleeves, and retaining hardware into machined bores.
Circlip Installation Retaining ring insertion for axial positioning.
Elastomeric Bushing Press-Fit Controlled interference fit of rubber-to-metal bushes into suspension linkages.
Thread-Locking Application Anaerobic adhesive application on threaded joints.
Leak / Torque Testing Validation of hydraulic fittings and threaded torque specifications.
VCI Packaging Volatile Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) oil application and vacuum-sealed export packaging.
