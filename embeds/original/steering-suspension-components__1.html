Why India Wins on Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

1. Quality Failure Cost Avoidance

Chinese forging suppliers operating below Tier-1 export standards have documented higher rates of:

  • Material mix-ups (incorrect steel grades)
  • Hardness non-conformance after heat treatment
  • Dimensional drift caused by limited SPC monitoring
  • Surface defects such as laps and folds escaping inspection

Indian IATF 16949-certified manufacturers such as Varsha Forgings follow globally accepted automotive quality systems. Preventing even a single production line stoppage or warranty claim can outweigh any apparent per-piece savings from lower-cost sourcing.

4. Currency & Tariff Stability

  • India–EU FTA negotiations may reduce duties on engineered goods.
  • India–UK trade cooperation supports preferential market access.
  • US tariffs on certain Chinese steel products have increased landed costs for Chinese forgings.
  • Indian Rupee exchange movements have generally shown lower volatility than RMB in recent market cycles.
